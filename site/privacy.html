Privacy policy

Effective date: <EFFECTIVE_DATE>

  • Your data stays on your computer and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive.
  • Google data is used only for sign-in (name and email) and for the app's own Drive folder, under the drive.file scope only: the app cannot see other files in your Drive.
  • API keys are stored encrypted in Windows Credential Manager and are sent only to the model provider you chose.
  • No server collects anything. There is no analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • The only network requests besides model providers and Google are anonymous update checks to GitHub.
  • The text you translate is sent to the model provider you chose and is governed by that provider's terms.
